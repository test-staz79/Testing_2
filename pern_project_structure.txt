Beginner Rule to Follow

When coding, ask yourself:

Am I writing SQL?
→ models

Am I handling req/res?
→ controllers

Am I writing app logic?
→ services

Am I defining endpoints?
→ routes

If you follow just this rule, your architecture stays clean.

For your portfolio projects (auth, dashboards, CRUD apps, AI app, SaaS-style apps), this structure is more than enough and won’t feel overengineered.
